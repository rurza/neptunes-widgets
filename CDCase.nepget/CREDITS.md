The CD Case widget is built on two third-party 3D models. Both are licensed
CC BY 4.0 <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>, which requires
attribution wherever the work is distributed, and requires that changes be
indicated. Both are named in the widget's own description, so the credit is on
the widget's card in Settings and on its page in the gallery, not only in here.

## Disc

"CD" by **Regex** — <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9dbad8f4c1ce472698595bd873067b48>
Licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: decimated, re-materialled, and the label
re-textured with the playing album's artwork.

## Case

"Hypnagogia 無限の夢 Boundless Dreams Jewel Case" by **sodaraptor**
<https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hypnagogia-boundless-dreams-jewel-case-20e4780167b6441fb364060c79870378>
Author: <https://sketchfab.com/sodaraptor>

Licensed CC BY 4.0. It is the jewel case from sodaraptor's game *Hypnagogia
無限の夢 Boundless Dreams*. Modified: the game's cover, spine and back artwork
are painted over at runtime, the promo insert and the model's own disc mesh are
removed, and the lid is closed (the model ships it open).

## Note on textures

CC BY covers the geometry and the textures the author made. It does not
launder any third-party material that rides along inside a model. Neither
model's original artwork is displayed by the widget: every printed surface is
repainted with the current album's artwork before the first frame.
